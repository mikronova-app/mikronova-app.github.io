What kids will find in Mikronova

🎮

Mini games

In every chapter your child does the organelle's job: make energy, ship packages, sort waste, catch sunlight for photosynthesis. All instructions are narrated.

📖

Audio stories

Every organelle has its own narrated story, page by page – great for kids who can't read yet and lovely at bedtime.

🧩

Jigsaw puzzles

5 colorful puzzles per chapter with big pieces for little hands.

🎨

Coloring pages

Large areas that are easy to color with a finger.

❓

Narrated quiz

5 questions at the end of every chapter, read aloud. Score 80 or more to earn a heart.

🌍

3 languages

English, German and Turkish – practice a new language while learning about the cell.
